\subsection{The design rule}

If you generate a reference image for a geometric consumer, do not train it under
plausibility pressure. The operational figure, on the production path of the delivered
tool: on 20 urban Ankara chips the L1-only generator reaches a positional error of
$0.593 \pm 0.041$~px (BT.601 gray, mean of eight stochastic draws per chip), better than
the published generator on 20 of 20 chips.

\subsection{Relation to the theory}

The result is an empirical instance of the perception--distortion trade-off
\cite{blau2018perception} in a geometric setting. The theorem is not contradicted; the
consumer is identified. A feature matcher lives on the distortion axis, so for a matcher
the perceptual end of the bound is the wrong end, and a loss that buys perceptual quality
pays in exactly the currency a matcher spends. What the factorial adds is not a
refinement of the theory but a statement about who pays for movement along its axis: each
of two plausibility pressures, the adversarial term and a perceptual reconstruction term,
is established separately as a positional penalty, at six seeds with the sign fixed in
advance. Nothing is claimed about how the two combine. The mechanism was predicted: Cohen
\emph{et al.}\ show that pursuing perceptual quality converts uncertainty about the
ground truth into confidently rendered false detail \cite{cohen2024looks}; an invented edge
is that false detail, instantiated as a control point.

The classical Cram\'er--Rao result for shift estimation, that variance scales inversely
with gradient energy so that sharper is better, holds for a correct model; ours is a bias
argument, not a variance argument, and deliberate blurring is already known to reduce
bias error in sub-pixel displacement estimation \cite{pan2013bias}.

\subsection{Limitations}

The institution's own matching software was never run against these arms, so every
number here is a proxy; the two matcher-independence registrations bound how much the
proxy choice matters, and they do not remove it. The discriminator of the published model
is not available, so every adversarial arm began from a seeded random discriminator, a
deviation from the published set-up that Section~IV-B shows is not what makes those arms
lose. OpenStreetMap's own positional accuracy was never separated from the model's error;
part of the residual attributed to the model is the map's, and the ceiling this implies is
unmeasured. The perceptual loss was computed with torchmetrics 1.9.0 against the
upstream's 0.11.0, and LPIPS implementations drift. Known-displacement recovery was not
run for the LPIPS arms. The training inputs under-represent forest relative to the
production render path, at a cost of roughly 0.6~px on forest-heavy chips. The harness of
the descriptor-family matcher test was not preserved, so four of its registered parameters
are reported as configured rather than as verified.

Five limitations arose after the design was fixed. The learning-rate asymmetry between the
arm families is disclosed and its consequence bounded in Section~II-A, but the bound is
chip-level, at one seed, and it tests the schedule on the non-adversarial arms only: it
does not establish that the adversarial arms would be unaffected by the reverse
manipulation, which was not run. The evidence behind the original blur-control and
corrected-georeferencing rows of Table~II did not survive, as the data-availability
statement details; both rows now rest on other evidence, and two registered outputs of
that package are permanently unrecoverable. Point-level common support cannot be
constructed for arms whose keypoints are detected independently, so equal-count truncation
is the only substitute, it is ranked by a post-treatment score, and no positional contrast
in this letter is free of post-treatment conditioning. The factorial's 130 chips are one
city; nothing here establishes transfer to the rest of Turkey, and the European set enters
only the matcher rows, never the registered positional contrasts. Three registrations were
found, after the fact, to state a set, a rule or a condition differently from the code
that implemented it: a branch text with no determinate referent, a poolability rule under
which one quantity vetoed ten, and a tie rule over four arms implemented over five. In each
the reading held either way; none was repaired, because a rule rewritten after seeing
which way it cuts is indistinguishable from one adjusted to pass, and all three are
disclosed in the corrections log.

Two caveats travel with results stated elsewhere. The null currency result in
Section~I is equally consistent with ``currency does not help'' and ``the map had not yet
recorded the change'', and separating them needs edit-history analysis that was not done.
And the edge ratio separates the restrained arm from the unrestrained ones without
ordering the errors within the unrestrained group (Section~III-K): invention is a
necessary condition, not a complete explanation.

\subsection{What generalises}

Not ``GANs are bad''. The claim is about the consumer: any loss that rewards
plausibility will fill the regions the conditioning input does not constrain, and any
downstream task that treats structure as evidence of location will pay for it. The same
test applies wherever a generated image is fed to a matcher: SAR-to-optical translation
used as a matching bridge, super-resolution applied before registration, and simulated
references generally. The rule has been reached independently for a different consumer:
in solar physics, non-adversarial training was found to reduce the hallucinations that a
physical-measurement consumer pays for \cite{sayez2025mitigating}. One scope boundary: at 10~m the premise for a generated reference
does not bind, because real cloud-free imagery is available and current (Section~I);
sub-metre is where it would, and this letter does not measure there. Every registration,
result and correction behind this letter is in the open study repository named in the
data-availability statement.
