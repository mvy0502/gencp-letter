The study repository (\url{https://github.com/mvy0502/gencp-validation}) holds, under \texttt{tubitak/docs/}, every registration, the corrections log, and the
per-chip artifacts the reported numbers derive from, each pinned by hash in
\texttt{tubitak/docs/evidence/MANIFEST.md}, with the frozen analysis scripts under
\texttt{tubitak/scripts/}.
Every separately registered body of evidence is named here rather than covered
generically, so that a reader looking for the evidence behind a specific claim finds it
named: the six-seed block (per-chip residuals, surviving-point counts and input-silent
edge ratios for all five arms at seeds 45--50, Section~III-A to III-D and III-F); the two-seed block at seeds 43--44 and the hardware gate, the registered poolability
comparison of Section~II-A, with its re-run of seed 43 (Section~II-A and III-C); the informative-mask test (Section~III-H and Table~II); the equal-count truncation and the minimum-match-count sweep (Section~III-G); the warm-up and learning-rate probes at seed 43,
one set of runs read under two registrations, with their training-loss logs
(Section~II-A and III-J); the six-seed training-loss trend (Section~III-J); the epoch sweep
behind the training-time curve, 24 cells at epochs 1, 2, 5 and 10 (Section~III-E), with
the 96 generator checkpoints it read held outside the repository and listed by hash; the
single-seed checkpoint sweep and the descriptor-family and matcher-family registrations
behind Table~II; the 260 seed-independent rasters the edge-ratio measurement reads; and the OSM-render
premise check (Section~III-L), 130 chip-level residuals and counts with its registration.

Three things are not available, and are stated here rather than in a footnote. First, the
alternative-explanations package that originally supported the blur-control and
corrected-georeferencing rows of Table~II: six of its seven registered checks and its veto
rule have no surviving per-chip artifact, the scripts that produced them were never
committed, and the generated imagery they consumed no longer exists, so the procedures
cannot be re-run. Two of its registered outputs---a point-floor sensitivity sweep at Ankara, which is that
lost package's own registered sweep and not the minimum-match-count sweep of
Section~III-G, and per-stratum gains---are permanently unrecoverable, and it cannot now be determined
whether they ran and went unreported or never ran. The 86\% scatter decomposition cited in
Section~IV-D is among the lost results, which is why that row is answered by design. Second,
the harness of the descriptor-family matcher test was not preserved, so four of its
registered matcher parameters are reported as configured rather than as verified. Third,
the generated images of the fine-tuned arms at each seed are not in the repository;
re-inferring them is a stochastic path, because test-time dropout is active by the
generator's design, so a re-run is a replication and not a reproduction. The generated images produced up to 26 August 2026, the training checkpoints of the
generating and confirmatory runs, and the 96 epoch checkpoints behind Fig.~1 are held in
a private backup, verified by hash from the copy, and are available on request. The
generated images of the 24 epoch cells scored on 13 September 2026 are not in that backup
and exist on one machine; this is stated so that the backup is not read as covering
them.
The 130 Ankara evaluation inputs were rendered from live map-API queries that were not
archived as a dated snapshot; whether they could be regenerated byte-identically has not
been tested, but the renders themselves are in the same backup and, in their warped form,
in the repository. The discriminator of the published
model is not available from its authors.
