The study repository (\url{https://github.com/mvy0502/gencp-validation}) holds every
registration, the corrections log, and the per-chip artifacts the reported numbers derive
from: per-chip residuals and surviving-point counts for all five arms at each of the six
confirmatory seeds and the two earlier seeds; the per-chip edge ratios on both masks; the
260 seed-independent rasters the edge-ratio measurement reads; the equal-count truncation
outputs; and the frozen analysis scripts, each pinned by hash in the registration that
freezes it.

Three things are not available, and are stated here rather than in a footnote. First, the
alternative-explanations package that originally supported the blur-control and
corrected-georeferencing rows of Table~II: six of its seven registered checks and its veto
rule have no surviving per-chip artifact, the scripts that produced them were never
committed, and the generated imagery they consumed no longer exists, so the procedures
cannot be re-run. Two of its registered outputs---a point-floor sensitivity sweep at Ankara
and per-stratum gains---are permanently unrecoverable, and it cannot now be determined
whether they ran and went unreported or never ran. The 86\% scatter decomposition cited in
Section~IV-D is among the lost results, which is why that row is answered by design. Second,
the harness of the descriptor-family matcher test was not preserved, so four of its
registered matcher parameters are reported as configured rather than as verified. Third,
the generated images of the fine-tuned arms at each seed are not in the repository;
re-inferring them is a stochastic path, because test-time dropout is active by the
generator's design, so a re-run is a replication and not a reproduction. Training
checkpoints and generated images are held in a private backup and are available on request.
The 130 Ankara evaluation inputs were rendered from a live map API with no dated archive
and cannot be regenerated; they are in the same backup. The discriminator of the published
model is not available from its authors.
