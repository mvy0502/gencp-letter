The study repository (\url{https://github.com/mvy0502/gencp-letter}) holds, under \texttt{tubitak/docs/}, every registration, the corrections log, and the
per-chip artifacts the reported numbers derive from, each pinned by hash in
\texttt{tubitak/docs/evidence/MANIFEST.md}, with the frozen analysis scripts under
\texttt{tubitak/scripts/}.
Every separately registered body of evidence is named here, so that a reader looking for
the evidence behind a specific claim finds it. Per-chip residuals, surviving-point counts and input-silent edge ratios for all five arms
at seeds 45--50 form the six-seed block (Section~III-A to III-D and III-F). The two-seed block at seeds 43--44 sits beside the
hardware gate, the registered poolability comparison of Section~II-A, with its re-run of
seed 43 (Section~II-A and III-C). The informative-mask test is under Section~III-H and
Table~II; the equal-count truncation and the minimum-match-count sweep under Section~III-G.
At seed 43, the warm-up and learning-rate probes are one set of runs read under two
registrations, kept with their training-loss logs (Section~II-A and III-J), as are the
six-seed training-loss trend and the generating run's seed-42 loss logs behind the
cross-platform comparison (Section~III-J). Behind the training-time curve, the epoch sweep has 24 cells at epochs 1, 2, 5 and 10
(Section~III-E); the 96 generator checkpoints it read
are held outside the repository and listed by hash. The single-seed checkpoint sweep and the
descriptor-family and matcher-family registrations behind Table~II are there, as are the 260
seed-independent rasters the edge-ratio measurement reads and the OSM-render premise check
(Section~III-L), 130 chip-level residuals and counts with its registration. In the only form
they were retained, the scope measurements of Section~I (scene availability over 24 extents
and the currency test), recorded as tables in the positioning results document with no
per-chip artifact, and the two-pixel common-point count of Section~II-D, recorded in the
common-support registration's table only, are named here as well.

Three things are not available. First, the
alternative-explanations package that originally supported the blur-control and
corrected-georeferencing rows of Table~II: six of its seven registered checks and its veto
rule have no surviving per-chip artifact, the scripts that produced them were never
committed, and the generated imagery they consumed no longer exists, so the procedures
cannot be re-run. Two of its registered outputs---a point-floor sensitivity sweep at Ankara, which is that
lost package's own registered sweep and not the minimum-match-count sweep of
Section~III-G, and per-stratum gains---are permanently unrecoverable, and it cannot now be determined
whether they ran and went unreported or never ran. The 86\% scatter decomposition cited in
Section~IV-D is among the lost results, which is why that row is answered by design. Second,
the harness of the descriptor-family matcher test was not preserved, so four of its
registered matcher parameters are reported as configured rather than as verified. Third, the generated images of the fine-tuned arms at each seed are not in the repository;
re-inference is not the only route to them, but for anyone who chooses it the path is
stochastic, because test-time dropout is active by the generator's design, so a re-run is a
replication and not a reproduction. The generated images produced up to 26 August 2026, the training checkpoints of the
generating and confirmatory runs, and the 96 epoch checkpoints behind Fig.~\ref{fig:epochs} are held in
a private backup, verified by hash from the copy, and are available on request. The
generated images of the 24 epoch cells scored on 13 September 2026 are not in that backup and exist on one machine.
The 130 Ankara evaluation inputs were rendered from live map-API queries that were not
archived as a dated snapshot; whether they could be regenerated byte-identically has not
been tested, but the renders themselves are in the same backup and, in their warped form,
in the repository. The discriminator of the published
model is not available from its authors.
