# The move of the record to gencp-letter — 14 September 2026 (P15)

**Decision and reason.** `gencp-validation` is the internship delivery and stays as delivered; rebranding it as the paper's repository would change what the institution received. The paper and its evidence record move to `gencp-letter`, which becomes public with the preprint. This reverses the "no merge in either direction" rule of the working notes. That rule existed because merging `tubitak-tr` into `gencp-validation` would delete 263 files and destroy the record; this merge runs the other way and deletes nothing. The rule's letter is broken, its purpose kept, and the rule is rewritten in `CLAUDE.md` here and in the GenCP working repository's `CLAUDE.md`.
