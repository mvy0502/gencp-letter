# Style corpus — manifest, committed before any measurement (P12 A.3, 14 September 2026)

Twelve open-access papers in the letter's subfield (remote sensing image matching and registration; image-to-image translation for remote sensing; adversarial reference generation), all obtained as arXiv PDFs. MDPI Remote Sensing was in the venue list but its site refuses automated fetches, so no MDPI paper is in the corpus; that is a limitation, not a choice. Two halves: **older**, written 2017–2022 and taken as human-written prose; **recent**, 2024–2025, which may itself contain language-model output and is included to capture current venue norms. The baseline is weighted toward the older half and the halves are reported separately wherever they differ.

Prose only is measured, in both the corpus and the letter: equations, tables, captions, references, author blocks, index terms and acknowledgements are dropped before anything is counted; the abstract is kept.
